The house · Dahab, South Sinai

Somewhere between the mountains and the sea, there is a house where time takes a different shape.

It looks like the house of a family you never met: brass keys on a board, a record left on the gramophone, net curtains moving on the balcony. Nothing here is new to you. That is the strange part.

3:16, always.Every clock in the house stopped at the same hour. The hands point east, toward the sea.

“It feels like I've been here before.”

One house.
Five rooms.

Walk through them in order, the way you would arrive: through the Parlour, past the Kitchen Table, into the Sitting Room, up to the Balcony. The Study is the only room where the clocks still run.

Dahab, by the hour

Outside, the day moves. Inside, the hour stays.

Look out of the window as the day goes by. The mountains change colour five times before dinner. The clock on the wall does not move at all.
